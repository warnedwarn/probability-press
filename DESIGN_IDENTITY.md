# Identity brief

- Mechanism: freeze a sourced probability before resolution, then compute a deterministic Brier score from a separately sourced outcome.
- Product metaphor: a forensic photographic light table with two documentary plates and one magnifying score lens.
- Voice: editorial, exact, skeptical of hindsight.
- Interaction: lock forecast in a modal, add outcome later, inspect both immutable digests side by side.
- Result: one large score under the lens, never a generic dashboard card grid.
- Evidence story: deployment, anchor, early-resolution rejection, final resolution, and stored readback.
